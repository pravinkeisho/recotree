The problem

Most cluster manufacturers can't afford to get certified

Hundreds of small units in the same cluster make near-identical products, and each one pays for the same tests, audits and paperwork on its own.

Duplicated cost

Repeated BIS testing, factory audits and documentation raise costs and delay market access for every unit.

No trusted trail

Labs, buyers and regulators can't trace samples, designs and certificates end to end, so every claim needs re-checking.

Labs out of reach

Most small units have no in-house lab, while existing testing capacity sits underused because coordination is manual.

Why now

Compliance rules now change every year

  1. 2024

    Cast Iron Products QCO notified

    Grey iron castings (IS 210) and related standards come under mandatory BIS licensing.

  2. Nov 2025

    20 raw-material QCOs withdrawn

    Metals, chemicals and polymers are dropped after a government review of the QCO regime.

  3. Jan 2026

    Machinery order withdrawn

    The Machinery & Electrical Equipment Omnibus Order and its Scheme X obligation are rescinded.

  4. Today

    Cast Iron QCO still in force

    Deadlines have passed, and most foundries in the Coimbatore cluster are not yet licensed.

Units can't tell what applies to them. RecoTree tracks which QCOs and IS codes cover each product and tells every unit what to do when the rules change.

How it works

From factory onboarding to live regulator oversight

A permissioned blockchain connects MSMEs, cluster associations, testing labs and regulators on one shared record.

  1. Onboard

    The cluster body and member units register digital factory profiles.

  2. Test

    The shared NABL or cluster lab uploads results straight to the chain, where they can't be edited.

  3. Comply

    Smart contracts check results against the IS code and compile each unit's licence file.

  4. Mark

    Every batch gets a QR code linked to its record, so buyers verify it in seconds.

  5. Monitor

    BIS sees a live dashboard. Alerts fire for test expiry, IS revisions and calibration.

The insight

BIS already lets one cluster lab serve every unit

The rule exists

Under the BIS Cluster Based Testing Facility (CBTF) provision, a shared cluster lab can act as each member unit's in-house testing facility for its own ISI mark licence.

The gap is trust

Each unit keeps its own licence and accountability. BIS needs confidence that shared-lab results are genuine, batch-linked and unaltered. RecoTree's on-chain record provides it.
